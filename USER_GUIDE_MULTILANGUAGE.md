## Changing Your Language

### During Registration

When creating a new account, you can select your preferred language:

1. On the **Registration** page, look for the **Language** dropdown
2. Select your preferred language (e.g., "🇫🇷 Français (FR)")
3. The interface will update to show options in your selected language
4. Complete your registration

### In Your Profile

To change your language anytime after signing in:

1. Go to **Profile** (usually in the top-right menu)
2. Find the **Language** or **Locale** selector
3. Click the dropdown and select your preferred language
4. The change is **automatic** - all pages will update immediately
5. Your preference is **saved** and will persist on next login

### Language Change Features

✅ **Instant Updates:** All text updates immediately  
✅ **Global:** Affects the entire application  
✅ **Persistent:** Your choice is remembered  
✅ **No Reload Needed:** Changes apply without refreshing

## Managing Currency Preferences

### Setting Your Default Currency

Your primary currency affects:

- How amounts are **displayed** across the app
- How **calculations** are shown (e.g., loan payments)
- Your **balance** display format

### Changing Your Currency

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Find the **Currency** selector
3. Select your preferred currency (e.g., "EUR - 100 €")
4. The currency preview shows how amounts will be **formatted**
5. Click **Save** to apply

### Example Currency Selections

When you select a currency, you'll see a **formatted preview**:

```
EUR - 100 €        (Euro - European format)
USD - $100         (US Dollar - US format)
CAD - $100         (Canadian Dollar)
AED - د.إ 100      (UAE Dirham - Arabic)
NGN - ₦100         (Nigerian Naira)
GHS - GH₵100       (Ghanaian Cedi)
ZAR - R100         (South African Rand)
XOF - CFA100       (West African Franc)
```

### Multi-Account Currency Support

If you have multiple accounts in different currencies:

- Each account displays in its **own currency**
- Your profile setting is your **default display currency**
- Transactions show the **actual currency** of each account

## Understanding Currency Formats

### How Amounts Display

Currency formatting depends on your **language/locale** setting:

#### Euro (EUR)

```
English (US):       €100.00  (symbol first)
English (UK):       €100.00  (symbol first)
French:            100,00 €  (number first, comma for decimal)
Arabic:            100 د.إ    (right-to-left)
Portuguese:        €100,00   (comma for decimal)
```

#### US Dollar (USD)

```
English (US):       $100.00  (symbol first)
English (UK):       $100.00  (symbol first)
French:            100,00 $  (number first)
Portuguese:        $100,00   (comma for decimal)
```

#### Thousands Separator

Large amounts use your locale's separator:

```
English:    1,234.56     (comma as thousands separator, period as decimal)
French:     1 234,56     (space as thousands separator, comma as decimal)
Portuguese: 1.234,56     (period as thousands separator, comma as decimal)
```

### Currency Codes

When a currency symbol isn't available, amounts show as:

```
AED 100      (UAE Dirham with code)
NGN 1,000    (Nigerian Naira with code)
```

## Tips & Best Practices

### For International Users

1. **Select Your Locale First:** Choose your language during registration for the best experience
2. **Check Currency Settings:** Ensure your default currency matches your primary account
3. **Review Date Formats:** Familiarize yourself with how dates appear in your chosen locale

### For Multi-Country Users

1. **Account in Different Currencies:** Create accounts in each currency you need
2. **Primary Currency Setting:** Set your most-used currency as your default
3. **Monitor Exchange Rates:** Check current rates when converting between currencies

### Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Currency symbols not showing correctly

- **Solution:** Ensure your language setting matches your locale
- **Example:** For EUR amounts, use French (fr-FR) or English (en-GB)

**Issue:** Dates showing in wrong format

- **Solution:** Check your Profile language setting
- **Verify:** Navigate to Profile and confirm the correct language is selected

**Issue:** Numbers showing with wrong separator

- **Solution:** Your locale determines the separator (comma vs period vs space)
- **Example:** French uses space for thousands: 1 000 (not 1,000)

## Common Questions & Answers

### Q: Can I change my language anytime?

**A:** Yes! Go to Profile and select a different language. Changes take effect immediately.

### Q: Will changing languages affect my data?

**A:** No. Changing your language only affects how text and numbers are displayed. Your data and transactions remain unchanged.

### Q: Do I need to save after changing language?

**A:** No. Language changes are automatic. Your preference is saved in the background.

### Q: Can my transactions show in different currencies?

**A:** Yes! Each account can hold a different currency. Amounts show in the account's currency, but your display format (symbols, thousands separators) depends on your locale setting.

### Q: What happens if my locale isn't supported?

**A:** The app will use English (en-US) as a fallback. Please contact support to request additional languages.

### Q: Do date conversions happen automatically?

**A:** Dates are displayed in your locale's format, but they represent the same moment in time. No conversion occurs.

### Q: Can I have different currency and language settings?

**A:** Yes! For example, you can use French language with EUR currency display, or English language with USD currency display.

### Q: How do I know my setting was saved?

**A:** In your Profile, the selected option shows your current setting. Your preference persists after logout.

### Q: Are there any delays when switching languages?

**A:** No. Language switching is instant. All pages update without requiring a page reload.

## Glossary

**Locale:** A combination of language and region (e.g., "fr-FR" = French-France)

**Currency:** The money type (EUR, USD, etc.)

**Formatting:** How numbers, dates, and amounts are displayed (symbols, separators, order)

**Internationalization (i18n):** Support for multiple languages and regions

**Localization:** Adapting content for a specific locale
